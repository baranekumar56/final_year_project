GPU-accelerated dataframe analytics

Operations: group-bys, joins, filters, window functions on big tabular data (like cuDF/pandas but distributed).​

Scenario: log analytics, clickstream analysis, time-series aggregation over millions of rows.

Real-time or near real-time analytics

Perform streaming aggregations (e.g., rolling fraud scores, anomaly detection) on batches of events coming in, using GPUs to keep latency low.​

You can demonstrate low-latency processing vs a CPU-only baseline.

Classical ML on big data

Distributed GPU-accelerated training or inference for tree-based models or linear models (not just deep learning), similar to RAPIDS cuML or Spark + GPU accelerators.​

Example: large-scale clustering, regression, or classification on tens of millions of rows.

Simulation / scientific workloads

GPU-accelerated Monte Carlo simulations or numerical models distributed across nodes, where each worker GPU runs many parallel trials.​

Easy to parallelize and measure speedup vs CPU.
